Quotient: tan(θ) = sin(θ) / cos(θ)
Pythagorean: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Double Angle (Sine): sin(2θ) = 2 sinθ cosθ
Double Angle (Cosine): cos(2θ) = cos²θ - sin²θ
Shortcut hint: [2sinθcosθ] / [cos²θ - sin²θ] = tan(2θ)
